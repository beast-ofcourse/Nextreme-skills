Executive Summary

North Region closed Q4 2025 with 98.2% SLA adherence — a 2.1-point gain over Q3. On-time delivery rose to 94.7% while incidents fell 18% QoQ. The single material risk remains cold-chain supplier concentration, addressed by the dual-vendor program (Section 4). Volume grew 7.3% to 412,800 shipments; cost per shipment fell $4.12 → $3.84.
