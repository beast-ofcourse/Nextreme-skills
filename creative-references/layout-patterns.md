# Layout Patterns

## 1. The Roadmap (Vertical Spine)
Best for learning paths (e.g., "How to learn Computer Science").

- **Structure**: A vertical line down the center.
- **Logic**: Sequential progress from top to bottom.
- **Branching**: Subtopics branch out to the left and right of the spine.
- **Visuals**: Use dashed lines for connectors to subtopics.

## 2. The Process Flow (Horizontal)
Best for business processes or algorithms.

- **Structure**: Left-to-right progression.
- **Logic**: Input -> Step 1 -> Step 2 -> Output.
- **Visuals**: Solid arrows with clear directionality.

## 3. The Decision Tree
Best for troubleshooting or complex logic.

- **Structure**: Top-down branching.
- **Logic**: Diamond nodes for questions, rectangular nodes for actions.
- **Visuals**: Labeled connectors (Yes/No).

## 4. The Taxonomy Map
Best for categorization.

- **Structure**: Central hub with radial or hierarchical clusters.
- **Logic**: Broad category -> Sub-categories.
- **Visuals**: Grouped boxes with background shading for clusters.
